# Scientific Validation & Benchmark Harness

TheraDOS includes a prospective benchmark framework to evaluate decision quality over time.

## Benchmarks
- **Benchmark A**: Historical/temporal holdout recovery (predicting known therapeutic targets from historical data).
- **Benchmark B**: Failure rejection (correctly assigning HOLD/TERMINATE to failed clinical programs based on hard gates).
- **Benchmark C**: Locked prospective predictions.
- **Benchmark D**: Decision efficiency (experimental cost & time to reach a conclusive ADVANCE or TERMINATE decision).
